# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

<p>简体中文 | <a href="CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md">English</a></p>

## Our commitment

Participating in this project should be possible for anyone, regardless of experience level, physical
ability, region, language, gender and expression, or personal background. This document states the floor,
not the virtues — **you are protected here even if you never contribute a line**.

Maintainers are responsible for keeping the environment discussable. Technical disagreement is welcome;
behavior aimed at a person is not.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Back a position with something checkable: code, spec, measured data, a document link.
- Admit what is unknown: label unverified conclusions as unverified; a machine PASS is not a human review.
- Argue with the claim, not the claimant; critique code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that others participate in different languages and phrasings. The Chinese and English copies
  saying the same thing is **our** responsibility, not the reader's.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, humiliation, mockery, targeted pressure, sustained interruption or takeover of a thread.
- Disclosing someone's personal information, credentials, or details of an unpatched vulnerability — publicly or privately.
- Treating "my time / my project matters more than yours" as a reason to demand service from others.
- Fabricating acceptance results, checking off items that were never executed, or using false green to hide
  problems. In this project that is also a conduct issue, because it directly damages the next person to
  pick the work up.

## Reporting and handling

- Contact the maintainer through a private channel (once the remote repository exists, Security / a private
  contact route). Do **not** describe the incident publicly in an Issue, PR or discussion.
- Include what happened, when and where, who was involved, and any evidence. Incomplete reports are still fine.
- Maintainers commit to acknowledging a report within 72 hours and to communicating the outcome after assessment.
- Both reporter and reported person keep their privacy; confidentiality is the default, not the exception.

## Enforcement

Maintainers escalate according to severity and history:

1. **Private reminder**: a one-off, non-public correction.
2. **Public reminder**: state the problematic behavior and the expectation in the discussion area, without name-and-shame.
3. **Temporary cooling-off**: suspend the participant's involvement in the affected thread/repository, with a
   stated duration and conditions for restoration.
4. **Termination**: repeated severe violations, or harm at the credential, privacy or security level.

The same standard applies to maintainers. If you believe a decision was unfair, say so directly — this
judgment is not meant to be unquestionable.
